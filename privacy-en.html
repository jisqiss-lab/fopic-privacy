The Tens (the "Company") establishes and publicly discloses this Privacy Policy pursuant to Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act ("PIPA") to protect the personal information of data subjects and to promptly and smoothly handle any related grievances.

This English version is provided for the convenience of users outside Korea. In the event of any discrepancy, the Korean version prevails.

Article 1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information being processed will not be used for purposes other than those specified below, and separate consent will be obtained in accordance with Article 18 of PIPA if the purposes of use are changed.

  • Account management — Identifying members through social login, providing frames and stickers saved to an account across multiple devices, and preventing fraudulent use
  • Service delivery — Storing and synchronizing frames and stickers created by users, and temporarily sharing frames, stickers, and videos via QR code
  • Paid service operation — Verifying and restoring subscription entitlements, and applying tier-based feature limits
  • Service improvement and reliability — Diagnosing app errors, responding to incidents, and analyzing usage statistics
  • Advertising — Displaying advertisements to free-tier users and measuring their performance. Personalized advertising is provided only where the user has separately permitted it.

Article 2. Categories of Personal Information Processed

Core features such as taking photos, editing, and saving to the device can be used without creating an account. Photos and videos captured or edited in this way are processed entirely on the user's device and are not transmitted to the Company's servers.

Automatically collected items

Information that may be automatically generated and collected during service use, including IP address, device information (operating system, device model), app version, service usage records, and error and fault records. Advertising identifiers (iOS IDFA, Android ADID) may also be collected for the purpose of serving advertisements.

Sensitive information and unique identifier information

The Company does not collect sensitive information under Article 23 of PIPA, nor unique identifier information under Article 24 (resident registration number, passport number, driver's license number, or alien registration number).

Regarding photographs containing faces — The Company recognizes that photographs taken or imported by users may contain faces. The Company does not use such photographs for the purpose of authenticating or identifying any specific individual, and does not generate biometric identification information from faces. Image processing that separates a subject from the background is performed on the user's device, and its output is not transmitted to the Company's servers. Device biometric authentication (such as Face ID) is performed by the operating system, and the Company receives only whether authentication succeeded.

Article 7. Destruction Procedure and Method

When personal information becomes unnecessary due to expiration of the retention period or achievement of the processing purpose, the Company destroys such information without delay.

  • Procedure — When a member requests account deletion from the app's settings screen, the Company first deletes the files stored by that member, then immediately deletes the account and all data linked to it. No grace period is applied.
  • Method — Personal information stored in electronic file form is permanently deleted using methods that prevent recovery or reconstruction.
  • QR sharing files — Files older than 24 hours from upload are deleted automatically, without requiring any separate request from the user.

Article 11. Personal Information of Children Under 14

The Company does not permit children under the age of 14 to create an account, and confirms at the time of registration that the user is aged 14 or older. Children under 14 may still use the core features of the app — capturing, editing, and saving photos — without an account; in that case, photos are processed only on the device and are not transmitted to the Company.

Because the Company does not collect dates of birth, it does not independently verify whether a user is a child. However, where the Company becomes aware that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected — through notification by a legal representative, disclosure by the user, or notice from a public authority — it destroys such information without delay.

Legal representatives may request access to or deletion of a child's personal information using the contact in Article 10. Upon verifying the account in question, the Company deletes that account and all data linked to it without delay and informs the requester of the outcome.

Article 12. Security Measures

The Company implements the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

  • Access privilege management — Row-level access controls are applied to the database so that each user can access only their own data.
  • Encryption in transit — All communication between the app and the servers takes place over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Secure storage of credentials — Login authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • Minimization of personnel — The number of personnel handling personal information is kept to a minimum.
  • Automatic deletion of shared files — QR sharing files are deleted automatically after 24 hours so that they are not retained unnecessarily.
